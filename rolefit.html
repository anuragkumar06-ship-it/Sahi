How RoleFit Works

Four steps.
Forty seconds.

RoleFit is not ChatGPT with a resume prompt. It is a multi-stage system that decomposes the JD, maps it to your verified record, regenerates a structured resume, and validates against Indian ATS schemas — all locked to facts that already exist in your Sahi profile.

Step 01
📋

Decompose

The JD is broken into role intent, must-haves, nice-to-haves, ATS keywords, and tone. We extract structured signals you'd never have time to read for.

Step 02
🔗

Map

Each JD signal is matched to facts in your verified Sahi record: prior roles, achievements, tools, languages, projects. No invention. Only re-arrangement of truth.

Step 03
✨

Regenerate

Bullet points are re-written, re-ordered, and re-framed to lead with the most relevant achievements. Voice, tense, and metric density are tuned to the role's seniority.

Step 04
🛡️

Validate

The output passes through six Indian ATS schemas (Workday, Taleo, Keka, Darwinbox, Zoho Recruit, Naukri RMS). You see a Fit Score and an apply-ready PDF.

RoleFit FAQ

Will it ever invent fake experience or skills?

Never. RoleFit can only re-arrange and re-frame facts already verified in your Sahi profile. If the JD asks for a skill you don't have, RoleFit will tell you — and recommend a Career OS module to build it. We literally cannot hallucinate, because the model is gated on your verified record at retrieval time.
How is this different from ChatGPT with a resume prompt?

Three ways. First, ChatGPT regularly fabricates achievements; we cannot. Second, RoleFit is pre-trained on Indian career archetypes and Indian ATS systems, so output is parsed cleanly. Third, RoleFit produces a quantified Fit Score so you know whether to apply at all — saving you from wasted applications.
Is my career data safe?

DPDP-Act native. Your verified record is encrypted at rest and never used to train shared models. RoleFit runs inference against your private record only. Read our privacy policy for the legal version.
What languages does RoleFit support?

English at full quality on Day 1. Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali, Marathi, Malayalam, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia, Assamese, Urdu in beta. Reaching all 22 scheduled languages by Year 2.
Can I export the resume?

Yes — PDF, DOCX, plain-text, and a copy-paste-ready version for online forms. Free users get PDF export. Premium users get all formats plus a recruiter-shareable Sahi profile link.
Does it write cover letters too?

Every RoleFit generation includes a tailored cover letter that mirrors the resume's framing and references specific JD points. Two-paragraph default; expandable to one page.
