The five-layer protocol

How a job becomes "Sahi Verified".

Five mandatory layers. No exceptions. Even our biggest enterprise customers go through every step.

1
Corporate KYC

CIN check via MCA21, GST verification, bank-account name match, signing director's PAN — all before posting day-1.

2
Recruiter KYC

Each individual recruiter inside the company has a personal KYC + corporate-email match. No anonymous DMs ever.

3
Job-spec audit

AI screen for forbidden phrases ("registration fee", "deposit"), salary-band sanity, and JD-vs-title mismatch.

4
Outcome telemetry

Every application tracked: response within 7 days? Rejection feedback? Offer issued? Live signals feed Trust Score.

5
Public Trust Score

0-100 score visible on every job card. Drops in real time on bad behaviour. Recovers via verified outcomes.

Real consequences · not theatre

What happens when an employer's Trust Score drops.

SCORE 80-100

Sahi Verified

Default placement. Eligible for outcome-linked pricing. Featured slots. Dedicated CSM at Trust 90+.

SCORE 60-79

On Watch

Yellow indicator on every JD. Auto-emailed actionable feedback. Featured slots withdrawn until recovery.

SCORE 40-59

Restricted

Cannot post new JDs. Existing JDs marked "candidates: review carefully". Mandatory remediation playbook.

SCORE < 40

Suspended

Removed from Sahi within 24 hours. Public Trust Ledger entry. Re-application requires fresh KYC + 90-day cooldown.
