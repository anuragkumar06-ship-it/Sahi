Employer FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What does the KYC verification process look like?

Three documents: GST registration, PAN, and a bank-account confirmation letter. Optionally, an authorized signatory's Aadhaar. Our Trust & Safety team turns it around in a 48-hour SLA. Once approved, you get a Sahi Verified Employer badge and dashboard access.
How is your Trust Score calculated?

Four inputs: response rate (30%), time-to-reply (25%), offer-vs-JD honesty (25%), refund/dispute history (20%). Computed weekly, visible publicly. Detailed methodology in our Trust Methodology paper.
Can I integrate Sahi with my existing ATS?

Yes — we have native two-way integrations with Workday, Taleo, Keka, Darwinbox, Zoho Recruit, and Greenhouse. SMB plan includes basic webhooks; Mid-Market and Enterprise plans include full bi-directional sync.
What if a Sahi-Verified candidate turns out to have lied?

Three remedies. (1) Full refund of any placement fee within 90 days. (2) The candidate's verification is publicly revoked. (3) We pause our certification standards for that company for 30 days while we investigate. Trust is bidirectional.
Can we trial Sahi alongside our current portal?

Yes — we encourage it. Our 60-day free pilot lets you run Sahi parallel to Naukri/LinkedIn/whatever you currently use. At day 60, you compare shortlist quality. If we don't win on hire-quality-per-rupee, you owe us nothing.
Do you serve startups outside India?

Today, India only. Our Verified Network depth, KYC infrastructure, and Mitra agent layer are India-native. We're considering Gulf and SEA expansion in Year 4 — happy to add you to the early-access list.
