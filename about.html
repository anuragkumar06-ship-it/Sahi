India does not have a job-portal problem. It has a job-trust problem. We started Sahi because every Indian we know has the same story: a fake recruiter call, a deposit that disappeared, a paid "premium" that delivered nothing. We're rebuilding job-tech around the only thing that should have ever mattered — honesty.

Our Origin

It started with one Reddit thread.

In April 2024, a 24-year-old engineer from Lucknow posted on r/LegalAdviceIndia. The headline: "How a major job portal trapped me into paying ₹42,000 in 60 minutes." The comments — hundreds of them — were variations of the same story. People had been quietly losing money to a category that was supposed to help them.

Three of us — an ex-fintech PM, a Tier-2 hiring manager, and a Bengaluru engineer with a cousin in rural Bihar — had all read that thread independently. We met for coffee that month and asked the only question that mattered: why has nobody built a job portal that simply tells the truth?

The answer was uncomfortable. The incumbents can't. Sixty per cent of their revenue comes from products that exist precisely because the platform isn't honest. To become honest, they would have to detonate the very monetization that funds them. We realized that "honesty" wasn't a feature you could bolt on. It was a category — and a new company would have to build it from scratch. We named that company Sahi. सही. Right. Correct. Honest.
