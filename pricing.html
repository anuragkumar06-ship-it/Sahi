Pricing FAQ

The questions we get most.

When does the Interview Success Fee get charged?

Only after a verified interview happens — confirmed by both you and the employer through Sahi's two-way attestation. If the interview is rescheduled, no fee. If the interview is a no-show by the employer, no fee. If you choose not to attend, no fee. The ₹499 is capped at one fee per role you apply to.
Why is Premium so cheap?

Because we make money on employer subscriptions, not seeker subscriptions. Career OS Premium at ₹1,499/year is priced to cover infrastructure, not maximize ARPU. We'd rather have 100M people on the platform paying ₹1,499 than 5M people paying ₹15,000.
Can I downgrade or cancel?

Any time. One click in your account settings. Pro-rated refund within seven days of payment. Your verified profile stays free forever — you don't lose your Career OS record by cancelling.
Do you offer education or NGO discounts?

Yes. Premium is 50% off for verified students (with valid college ID). NGOs get our SMB plan at ₹0/year. Government departments get our Enterprise plan at a custom rate negotiated with each state.
Are there annual or multi-year discounts?

Premium is annual-only at ₹1,499 (no monthly option, because we don't want to lock anyone in). Employer plans get 10% off on a 24-month contract and 15% off on a 36-month contract. Talk to our team if you want a multi-year commit.
Does the Sahi Verified employer badge cost extra?

No. The badge is included in every employer plan. We don't gate trust signals behind a paywall — that would defeat the entire point.
