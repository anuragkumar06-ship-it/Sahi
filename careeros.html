Common questions

Career OS, demystified.

Who actually owns my Career OS?
You do — completely. We are a custodian, not the owner. You can export everything as signed JSON or PDF, share with any third party via OAuth, or permanently delete with cryptographic proof. DPDP-Act compliant from day one.
What if my old employer no longer exists?
We support fallback verifications — old payslips (encrypted, optional), bank-statement salary credits, EPFO records, or two co-signed peer recommendations from verified colleagues. Your truth doesn't depend on whether your employer is still around.
Is this just LinkedIn with extra steps?
No. LinkedIn is a self-declared profile. Career OS is a verified record — every event is co-signed by the entity that can vouch for it (employer HR, university, certificate provider). Recruiters see "Sahi-verified" badges; LinkedIn endorsements are noise by comparison.
Does Sahi sell my data?
Never. Read our Trust Methodology. We are a marketplace, paid by employers — never by your data. Anonymized aggregate insights (e.g., "PM salaries in Bengaluru +18% YoY") are published, but never anything that can identify you.
How much does Career OS cost?
₹0. Forever. Owning your career record is a right, not a product. We monetize verified-employer subscriptions and RoleFit Pro — see pricing.
